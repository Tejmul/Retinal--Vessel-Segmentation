Literature Review

Liu et al. (2022), FR-UNet
  • Keeps a full-size path through the whole network.
  • Nothing is lost to down-sampling, so one-pixel vessels survive.
  • Adds a two-threshold step that reconnects weak vessels.
  • Limitation: pinned old libraries make it hard to run today.
Dong and Deng (2024), MDFI-Net
  • Deformable convolutions inside a pulse-coupled block.
  • Keeps faint vessels visible in the deeper layers.
  • Reported 97.91–98.16% accuracy over three datasets.
  • Limitation: no code was ever released.

Literature Review

Qi et al. (2023), DSCNet
  • Sampling points curve along the vessel instead of a square grid.
  • Loss charges the model directly for broken shapes.
  • Reported 82.06% Dice on DRIVE; ICCV 2023.
  • Limitation: changes the convolution and the loss together.
Khan et al. (2023), MRC-Net
  • Blends features taken at several scales.
  • Trains against a discriminator, rewarding whole regions.
  • Published in Neural Networks (Elsevier).
  • Limitation: no public code to check the numbers.
5

Literature Review

Guo et al. (2026), SA-UNetv2
  • Attention added at every skip link, not only the bottleneck.
  • Loss pairs weighted cross-entropy with a correlation term.
  • 82.82% F1 from 0.26M weights; about 1s on a plain CPU.
  • Limitation: very new, little independent checking yet.
Liu et al. (2025), MM-UNet
  • Mamba-style state-space layers replace pointwise convolutions.
  • Aimed at thin, branching shapes that vary across images.
  • Reports +1.64% F1 on DRIVE; code released.
  • Limitation: the gain is relative, not an absolute score.
6

Literature Review

Mehmood et al. (2024), LVS-Net
  • Light multi-scale encoder with focal modulation at the bottleneck.
  • Dice 86.44% using 0.71M parameters, 2.74 MB.
  • Built for machines with very little compute.
  • Limitation: preprint, and no code was published.
Mehmood et al. (2025), LFRA-Net
  • Focal and region-aware attention in selective skip links.
  • Dice 84.28% with only 0.17M parameters.
  • Code is public on GitHub.
  • Limitation: preprint; almost no independent use so far.
7

Literature Review

Wu et al. (2024), Neuron programming
  • Searches over neuron types rather than over layouts.
  • A small helper network guesses good settings, keeping the search cheap.
  • Tested on DRIVE, STARE, CHASE_DB1 and HRF.
  • Critical question: does neuron-level search pay off on 20 training images?

Base Paper

Zhou, Yu and Shi (2021) — SGL
Study group learning: improving retinal vessel segmentation trained with noisy labels · MICCAI 2021
  • Argues the ceiling is the labels: many thin vessels were never marked.
  • Trains k models on different subsets, merges them into cleaner labels, retrains.
  • Main strength for us: data, labels and weights all ship in the repository.
  • The loss is a command-line flag, so our one change stays one change.

Overall findings
and next step

  • Reported F1 sits between 82.06% and 86.44% — a narrow band.
  • Those numbers cannot be compared directly: masking, thresholds and patch sizes all differ.
  • Four of the ten papers publish no code, so nobody can check them.
  • Every method trains pixel by pixel, and not one reports a connectivity score.
  • Next: reproduce SGL, then swap its loss for centre-line clDice and measure what moves.
